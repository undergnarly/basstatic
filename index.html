BASSTATIC x ANNA MORGAN

Friday, 4 September 2026 · Block 42, Nuanu, Bali
From 6 PM

Anna Morgan makes her Bali debut with Basstatic. Expect a fearless trip through footwork, jungle, UK garage, dancehall and breaks, powered by the Basstatic Sound System.

🎟️ Indonesia Resident Rp100K · Presale Rp250K · At the Door Rp350K

Anna Morgan

Bali Debut · Footwork · Jungle · UK Garage

New York-born and now based in Chiang Mai, Anna Morgan is a DJ, producer and curator with Jamaican and Okinawan roots. Her sound is grounded in soundsystem culture and moves freely across footwork, jungle, UK garage, dancehall and breaks.

She curates The Footwork Jungle events and Drumz Plz, pushing underground dance music through technical precision, adventurous selections and respect for its roots. This night marks her first appearance in Bali.

Joining her are Bali fast-bass specialist Mairakilla and Basstatic selectors Roba Grow and Muvs. No BPM ceiling — just room energy and serious music.

What is Basstatic

Basstatic is a cultural movement centered around bass, movement, and inner state. A space where music becomes a tool for release, expression, and transformation. Through deep bass and conscious movement, the dance becomes a form of meditation, a reset, and a way to return to an authentic state.
