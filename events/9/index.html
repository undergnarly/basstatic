Basstatic Returns

RASA x DAWN RAZOR

Friday, 16 October 2026 · Hue, Nuanu, Bali
From 6 PM

This time, we bring together three forces built for the system: heavyweight bass and percussion from Rasa, boundary-pushing club music from Dawn Razor, and the Basstatic Crew on home-system duty.

Three Forces

No Genre. Just Pressure.

Rasa is a Punjab-born DJ and engineer channeling soundsystem culture through high-energy, genre-crossing sets. Heavy low end, intricate percussion and a route through jungle, drum & bass, grime and electro.

Dawn Razor moves between techno, breaks, electro, mutated bass and post-trance. With more than two decades in music and releases across R&S, ARTS and beyond, his sets are built to dissolve genre borders.

Basstatic Crew completes the system: Vokvi, Roza Mozes, Muvs and Roba Grow.
