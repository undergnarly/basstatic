BASSTATIC x ANNA MORGAN

Friday, 4 September 2026 · Block 42, Nuanu, Bali
From 6 PM

Anna Morgan makes her Bali debut with Basstatic. Footwork, jungle, UK garage, dancehall and breaks on the Basstatic Sound System.

Anna Morgan

Bali Debut · Footwork · Jungle · UK Garage

New York-born and Chiang Mai-based, Anna Morgan is a DJ, producer and curator with Jamaican and Okinawan roots. Grounded in soundsystem culture, her sets cross footwork, jungle, UK garage, dancehall and breaks with fearless precision.

Joining her are Bali fast-bass specialist Mairakilla and Basstatic selectors Roba Grow and Muvs.

Line-Up

Anna Morgan · Mairakilla · Roba Grow · Muvs
